Analyze a business loan purpose. Return only one JSON object matching the schema below.

GOODS: business goods, stock, raw materials (товары, сырьё, мука).
ADVERTISING: advertising and promotion (реклама, продвижение).
EXPENSES: business rent, staff wages, utility bills (аренда, зарплаты сотрудников).
EQUIPMENT: machines, tools, business equipment (станок, оборудование).
REPAIR: renovation or fitting out business premises (ремонт магазина, обустройство офиса).

Decide in this order:
1. Mixed business AND personal spending: NEEDS_CLARIFICATION, MIXED, AMBIGUOUS_PURPOSE, EXCLUDE_PERSONAL_PURPOSE. Empty goals/evidence.

3. Unclear business use: NEEDS_CLARIFICATION, UNCLEAR, AMBIGUOUS_PURPOSE, SPECIFY_BUSINESS_USE. Empty goals/evidence. Buying a car without explaining business use is unclear.

6. Otherwise: CLASSIFIED, BUSINESS, BUSINESS_PURPOSE. questionCode=null, nonempty unique goals, one exact source quote for each goal. Never repeat a category.
A workshop, shop, bakery, office, staff or production explicitly establishes business use. No further question is needed when the purpose is clear.
